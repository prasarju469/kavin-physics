# Concept video selection

Selection date: 27 October 2026. The titles and creator descriptions below were retrieved from YouTube search results and matched to the supplied chapter. This is a first selection for the local preview, not a claim that every video has been watched or that every runtime is under a fixed limit. The pendulum embed was tested and exposes playback controls; its displayed runtime is 6:13. Full content review remains pending.
